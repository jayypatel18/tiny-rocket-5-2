# The cuts: who owns which, and the director's storyboard in words

Phase 2 of the night. The assets exist (panda, hill set, sky, rocket, effects). Now every cut gets ONE owner who builds its module `ROOT/tr2/shots/<name>.py` end to end: layout, camera, layers, acting, effects. Read `ROOT/tr2/shots/common.py` for the contract (`render(fl, n, f)` returns the picture before the finishing pass) and helpers (`cue`, `twos`, `sky_pic`, `shake`, `drift`, `dof`). Times below are film seconds; get them at run time with `common.cue(name, shot)` so that the picture follows `tr2/timeline.py` (the sound is built from the same file). Test with `python3 ROOT/render.py --still F`, `--sheet <shot> 12` and `--shot <shot> --scale 0.5 --jobs 2`.

Owners: A panda: s1_knot, s4b_goggles, s5a_match, s5c_breath, s7b_wonder, plus `tr2/acting.py` (poses for the small panda in the wide cuts, called by the other owners). B hill: s2_hill, s3_night, s4a_wake, s9b_out. C sky: s7a_clouds, s8_star. D fx: s5b_fuse, s6_lift, s9a_home. Director: s9c_paint, s9d_title, the cut list, dissolves, finishing. Use each other's modules through their documented APIs; if an API is missing something you need, ask its owner through the director (say so in your report) and meanwhile work around it.

Rules for every cut
- Frame 0 of every cut is already in motion. Nothing ever freezes except the stillness beat in s5c.
- Key action on ones, the rest on twos (`common.twos(fl)` as the `drawing` number and as the time you sample poses at). Camera moves are always on ones.
- Every camera move is motivated and eased; held cameras still breathe (`common.drift`). At least 4 depth layers in every wide cut.
- The panda and the rocket must read clearly: check the silhouette against the background values, add a soft contact shadow on the ground, and let the grass overlap feet.
- A cut that dissolves out (s2, s3, s8) must keep rendering for 16 frames past n.
- Cost under about 6 s a frame on one core.

## The storyboard
**s1_knot** (0.0 to 6.0, dusk, owner A). Close, the panda in three quarter view facing screen left, right of centre, head and shoulders and paws filling the frame (s about 6). A bamboo leg of the rocket enters from the lower left as a diagonal, lashed with string. Golden rim light from the low sun on the left. Behind: the hill grass and the valley, far out of focus, bokeh discs of pollen and the first fireflies drifting. Frame 0 is mid pull: string end clenched in its teeth, both paws hauling the knot tight, body leaning back, the string taut and trembling. 0 to 1.2 strain with tiny trembles. `knot_tug` 1.25: the quick tug, on ones, with a smear frame; the head jerks back, the ears whip forward then back. 1.6 it lets the string drop from its mouth. `ear_flicks` 1.9 and 2.3. `tail_swish` 2.6: the tail sweeps through the background. 2.8 to 3.5 it leans in to inspect the knot, pats it. `blinks` 3.6 and 4.1. Then pleased: eyes close into happy arcs, a small smile, chest out, `pleased_breath` 4.6. Hold alive to the cut. Camera: slow push in (zoom 1.00 to 1.08) with breathing.

**s2_hill** (6.0 to 14.0, dusk, owner B). Wide. Camera starts at about (1480, 930) zoom 1.12 and dollies right to about (1700, 930) easing out, so the foreground grass and the tree sweep past the far valley (strong parallax). Sky with drifting golden clouds, first stars arriving (stars 0 to 0.25). `village_lights`: windows come on one by one from 7.0 to 13.5. Long shadows of the tree, rocket and panda across the grass. The panda begins beside the rocket (x about 1570) facing it, walks three steps backward (`steps_back`) looking up at it, stops near x 1440, fists on hips, proud (`admire` 9.0), tail swaying. Then it turns and walks to the star map (`steps_to_map`, to x about 1250), leans in, raises a paw, taps the map (`map_rustle` 12.2, the paper flutters), and looks up at the first stars (13.0). Dissolves into night.

**s3_night** (14.0 to 21.5, night, owner B). Begins tilted up at the star field (camera cy about 250), the sky visibly turning (`star_turn`), the milky way, the `shooting_star_night` crossing the upper frame. From 14.6 to 19.0 the camera tilts down (eased) and pushes in a little to the hilltop: the panda asleep, curled against the rocket legs under a patchwork blanket, in moonlight with a blue rim, a few village lights still on below, mist in the valley, fireflies drifting through the grass. The blanket rises and falls with its breath. `ear_twitch_sleep` 19.3. Dissolves into first light.

**s4a_wake** (21.5 to 25.0, predawn, owner B, acting by A). Mid wide, zoom about 1.5 on the rocket and the panda at its feet, cold blue light, a rose band low behind the valley, mist. `wake` 21.8 eyes open. 22.0 it sits up, the blanket slides off. `yawn_stretch` 22.4: a huge yawn, arms up, squash and stretch, a head shake. 23.0 it looks up at the rocket, decides, and climbs: `climb_paws` 23.4 and 23.9, on ones, quick and light. `into_cup` 24.5: it swings over the teacup rim and drops in; the tail goes last and flicks out of sight.

**s4b_goggles** (25.0 to 28.5, predawn, owner A). Outside the teacup looking in: the porcelain wall fills the frame, the brass rimmed round window in the centre (about 760 px across), the panda's face behind the glass. Rack focus: the cut opens sharp on the rim, rivets and the reflections on the glass with the face soft, and racks to the face by 25.5. `goggles_snap` 25.7: both paws pull the goggles down over the eyes, they overshoot and settle, the ears bounce. 26.3 to 26.8 whiskers tremble, eyes dart. `swallow` 26.9: a nervous swallow, the chin bobs. 27.4 a breath in, the brows set. `nod` 27.8: one small determined nod, on ones, ears following. Reflections of the dawn sky slide slowly across the glass.

**s5a_match** (28.5 to 30.5, predawn, owner A). Close on the teacup rim running across the lower third; the paw with a match, the face above watching. `match_strike` 29.0: the paw sweeps the match along the rim (one smear frame), a flare bursts (sparks, glow), warm light blooms across the face and the cup, then settles to a small flame. 29.6 the match moves to the fuse end hanging over the rim on the left. `fuse_lit` 30.0: the fuse catches and the fizz runs down out of frame; the eyes follow it.

**s5b_fuse** (30.5 to 33.0, predawn, owner D). Ground level, macro. Large soft grass blades in the foreground, the fuse string lying through the grass toward the rocket's bamboo legs in the distance at the right. The spark head fizzes along the fuse from left to right in hard held steps (it holds two or three frames, then jumps), spraying motion blurred sparks, trailing a thin thread of smoke, leaving the fuse behind it black. Its warm light flickers on the nearby blades. The camera tracks with it on ones, lagging then catching up, and the focus racks from the spark to the rocket as it nears the legs.

**s5c_breath** (33.0 to 36.0, predawn, owner A). Close on the face through the window, tighter than s4b. `eyes_shut` 33.2: the eyes squeeze shut hard, the ears flatten, the cheeks puff with held breath, the paws grip the rim. A warm flicker from the fuse plays on the glass and dies at `fuse_end` 33.9. `stillness` 34.0 to 35.6: total stillness, the drawing does not move at all (no boil, no drift), matching true silence in the sound. `one_eye_opens` 35.3: one lid lifts over three frames, the pupil slides down and to the side. In the last two frames the first orange flicker touches the glass.

**s6_lift** (36.0 to 43.0, launch, owner D). The showpiece: ONE continuous camera move. Low and wide near the ground, about (1700, 980) zoom 1.35, big foreground grass. `ignition` 36.3: a one frame white flash, fire bursts from the nozzle, simulated smoke rolls outward along the ground both ways in a ring, the grass flattens outward in a ring, sparks and torn leaves fly, the rocket squashes down on its legs. `shudder` to 37.6: growing shake of rocket and camera, fins rattling. `liftoff` 37.6: it leaps (stretch), the legs dangle, the camera tilts up after it, lagging, then catching up. `passes_tree` 39.0: the canopy whips in the blast, leaves torn loose. `birds_burst` 39.5: the flock bursts from the branches (`tr2/birds.py`, written by the director: `birds.Flock(points_in_canopy)` and `birds.draw_flock(flock, cam, t, f, None)`). The hill drops out of the bottom of the frame, the smoke column glows orange from inside, the sky scrolls to the zenith, the first clouds pass (`into_first_clouds` 42.0), the rocket shrinks as it climbs away and punches into a cloud bank on the last frames.

**s7a_clouds** (43.0 to 46.5, sunrise, owner C). The rocket (D's `rocket.draw`, flame and trail from `fx`) climbs in the centre of the frame while cloud layers stream downward with parallax; it shakes, the fins rattle. The light climbs from blue grey shadow through rose to gold. Wisps whip past in front of the lens. `break_into_open_sky` 46.2: it bursts through the last deck in a spray of mist, the sun flares from the right, god rays fan out.

**s7b_wonder** (46.5 to 50.0, sunrise, owner A). Inside the teacup, close. `wind_face`: cheeks pushed back, fur and ears streaming, mouth pulled wide, eyes squeezed, hard vibration on ones, the window behind streaking with cloud. 48.0 the shaking eases, gold light sweeps across the face from the right. `wonder` 48.4: the eyes open huge with big gold highlights, the mouth a small o that turns into a smile, the fur settles, the ears lift. Slow push in.

**s8_star** (50.0 to 58.5, sunrise, owner C, star and trail by D's `fx`). The vista. A thin white trail rises from the cloud sea on the left. `star_ignites` 51.0: it ignites (flash, bloom, flare) into a brilliant shooting star that arcs across the whole sky from upper left to the right (`star_arc`), fast at first then gliding, a long luminous trail with glitter falling, its light sliding over the cloud tops and glinting in the rivers. Camera: slow majestic push in with a slight pan after the star. Dissolves to morning.

**s9a_home** (58.5 to 62.0, morning, owner D). Hilltop, wide, fresh morning. The scorched rocket drifts down under a patched paper umbrella parachute, swinging like a pendulum, the camera tilting down with it. `touchdown` 61.3: the legs touch, squash, a bounce, a settle; the umbrella folds down to one side with follow through; grass puffs; a wisp of smoke from the nozzle.

**s9b_out** (62.0 to 65.0, morning, owner B, acting by A). Mid, zoom about 1.7 on the rocket. `climb_out` 62.4: the panda's head pops up over the teacup rim, sooty faced, goggles askew; a head shake puffs soot. It climbs out and hops down (`paws_grass_home`), stands, and `looks_up` 64.2 at the sky where one star is still fading (`sky.render(..., fade_star=...)`); the camera tilts up a little to hold the star and the panda together.

**s9c_paint** (65.0 to 67.5, morning, director). Close on the star map (`tr2/starmap.py`). A paw with a dab of gold paint on one toe enters from the lower right, touches the paper at `brush_stroke` 66.4 and paints a tiny star at the end of the dotted flight path, then lifts away. The paper flutters.

**s9d_title** (67.5 to 74.0, morning, director, acting by A). The panda beside the map and the rocket. `looks_at_us` 67.9, a small smile. `tail_curl` 68.6. The camera drifts up to give the sky room; the title brushes in (`tr2/title.py`), the hanko presses, the small line follows. Fade to paper, then black.
