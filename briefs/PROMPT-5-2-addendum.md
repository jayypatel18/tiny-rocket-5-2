## Fable 5.2 brief

This section was added on top of the original story text above for the Fable 5.2 version. The story is the same. The bar is higher. The film must be made entirely by Fable 5.2, from an empty folder, without reading the Fable 5.1 code.

- **Animation:** full animation on ones for the key action (the knot, the fuse, ignition, lift off, the window), on twos elsewhere; real anticipation, overlap and follow through; squash and stretch on the panda; secondary motion on tail, ears, paper, smoke and grass. No sliding or tweened paper doll motion: every drawing rebuilt per frame.
- **Camera:** multiplane parallax with at least 4 depth layers, motivated camera moves (push in, pan, tilt up with the rocket, a rack focus), one long continuous move in the launch.
- **Picture:** painted backgrounds with real light (golden hour to night to dawn), atmospheric perspective, bloom and light shafts, a sky with moving clouds and stars, smoke and fire simulated (particles or a 2D fluid), sparks with motion blur. A consistent palette and a final grade.
- **Sound:** a real score with a melody that returns at the climax, built up voice by voice (piano, strings, woodwind, choir pad), foley for every action (paper, string, fuse hiss, ignition roar, wind), stereo with movement, a clean mix at about -14 LUFS with true peak under -1 dBTP, and silence used on purpose before ignition.
- **Length and format:** 60 to 75 seconds, 24 fps, master at 1920x1080 (or higher, then downsampled). The first frame must already be moving and beautiful (X autoplays muted). End card brushes "Fable 5.2".
- **The hook must stay true:** every frame and every sound is code the model wrote. No image models, no video models, no stock footage, no samples, no font files, no 3D assets downloaded from anywhere. Libraries for math, rendering and audio DSP (numpy, scipy, numba, Pillow) are fine.
